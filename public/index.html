How to use the microphone

When the timed drill starts, allow microphone access if prompted. Say “calm” to calm a panicking person. Reassurance now succeeds on the first attempt.

The microphone listens for the word “calm” when speech recognition is supported. You can say something reassuring such as “Stay calm, follow the open exit.” It does not control the doors.

If you cannot use the microphone or do not allow access, press Reassure once to calm a person instead. Selecting someone is optional; otherwise the button chooses a panicking person for you. The microphone is only used during the timed drill.
