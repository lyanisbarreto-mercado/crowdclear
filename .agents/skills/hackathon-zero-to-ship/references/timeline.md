# Timeline and time budgets

Plan backward from the actual deadline and all portals. These are example latest checkpoints for a full event, not budgets to spend or a required sequence. Deploy earlier when the first slice is ready and authorized.

| Checkpoint | 36 h event | 24 h event |
|---|---|---|
| Rules checked, brief/wedge recorded, PLAN ready | +30 min | +20 min |
| Runnable scaffold + required checks + health | +2 h | +1.5 h |
| First critical sandbox mechanic verified | +3 h | +2 h |
| Both beats twice locally | +8 h | +5 h |
| Judged deployment and phone path verified | +10 h | +6 h |
| **Feature freeze** | T-11 h | T-7 h |
| Stable stills and footage captured | T-8 h | T-5 h |
| Video uploaded, when authorized | T-6 h | T-4 h |
| Writeup and claims audit ready | T-4 h | T-2.5 h |
| **All portals submitted and verified** | T-1.5 h | T-1 h |

Before coding is allowed: rules, brief, sponsor research and permitted account setup only. Do not generate a scaffold to get around event start rules.

## Budget the unknowns

- Default initial research/brief budget: 15-30 min, focused on rules, one critical provider assumption and product direction. Record unanswered low-impact details and continue.
- Timebox a provider spike to 10-20 min, shortening with the remaining time. After two genuinely failed integration attempts, diagnose briefly, simplify, switch or cut.
- Start asset planning and submission copy alongside independent build work. Reserve time for export, upload, portal errors and required human steps.
- With less than 6 h remaining, plan from time left: roughly 5% rules/brief, 50% verified signature path, 15% hardening, 15% assets, 15% upload/submission buffer. These are adjustable ceilings; do not consume a needed upload buffer with research. Keep at least 15 min for final portal work when available, and a feasible explicit buffer even in a shorter session.

## Handoffs and sleep

Sleep in shifts where there is a team. Before handoff, push when authorized and record the current row, commit, checks, blockers and next step in PLAN or the assigned task note. Release ownership explicitly. An old timestamp alone never authorizes takeover: check the owner/agent and repository state; use a disjoint task or isolated worktree while unresolved.

## Behind schedule

When a checkpoint is missed materially:

1. Replan from now to the deadline and report the new targets. Stop dependent depth work while preserving useful independent lanes.
2. Cut speculative integrations, polish and depth first. Keep both demo beats by default. If one must go, record that cut and its consequence; never claim the missing beat works.
3. Preserve a verified signature moment, judge path, honest evidence, assets and submission buffer. Remove cut claims/surfaces from UI, README, `/judge` and pitch.
4. Keep pending external/human actions visible. A local package, fixture or portal draft is not live integration, uploaded video or submitted entry.
